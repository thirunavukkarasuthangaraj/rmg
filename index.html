A message from our founder

MJF Ln. R. Murugesan, B.Sc.Founder, RMG Enterprisses · Cabinet Adviser, Lions Dist 324J

In 1983 I founded RMG Enterprisses on one simple promise — honesty in every deal. I come from a humble rural background and came to Chennai to study; after my B.Sc., I followed my passion for land and property.

What began as a small venture has grown, over more than 40 years, into a real estate business that thousands of families trust. As an active Lions Club member and Cabinet Adviser, I believe business should give back to the community — that spirit of service, trust and care is the foundation of everything we do.

“Our name is on every job we do — and that is our promise to you.”
